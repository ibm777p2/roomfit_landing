Why I'm building this

When I moved to San Francisco, I had no network and no rental history here. I tried Craigslist and nearly got scammed. Facebook groups were the best option, and every listing was a stranger I had to guess about.

Later, as the one listing a room, it took 4–5 weeks to find someone. So I'm building the tool I kept wishing existed.
